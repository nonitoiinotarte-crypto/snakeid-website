SAFETY NOTICE

Never rely on a photo alone.

Keep your distance. Do not touch, corner, or attempt to capture a snake. Treat an unidentified snake as potentially venomous. If bitten, seek emergency medical care immediately; keep still and follow local emergency guidance. Do not cut the wound, suck out venom, or apply a tourniquet.
